Price Management Hub

User & Operator Manual

This manual covers the centralized pricing engine for the quotation and CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) system. It enables your organization to configure static price books, quantity-based slab pricing, customer-specific discounts, promo coupons, and multi-currency conversion sheets.

🧭 Key Concepts

The system uses a hierarchical pricing engine to compute the final price of an item when generating a quotation:

How it layers: When a salesperson adds a product to a quotation, the engine starts with the product's base catalog price, checks for active Price Books, layers on quantity-based slab rules, and then applies stackable discounts and promotional codes.

🗂️ Core Pricing Modules

The Price Management Hub is divided into 5 core tabs:

  • Price Books: Catalogs of custom product rates that override default prices for specific segments (Standard, Customer Specific, Region Specific, Dealer/Partner Specific, or Project Specific).
  • Pricing Rules: Rules designed for volume-based pricing or specific group markups (e.g. Slab tiers based on quantity).
  • Discount Policies: Company-wide structural policies that run automatically during calculations. Can be percentage-based or flat rates, and set as stackable.
  • Promotions & Coupons: Code-activated marketing campaigns (e.g., SUMMER26) with usage limits and dates.
  • Currency Sheets: Live ledger mapping conversion rates from your base currency (INR) to foreign currencies (USD, AED, EUR) for international quotes.

📖 Step-by-Step Guides

1 How to Create a New Price Book

Go to Master ➔ Price Management in the sidebar. Click the Add Config button, fill in the Book Name, Book Type (e.g. Region or Customer), and input target values (such as state code KA). Click Save.

2 How to Map Rates to a Price Book

Click Manage Rates on the card of your Price Book. In the modal, select a product from the list, input the custom rate, and click Map Rate. The custom rate will now override default catalog prices automatically.

3 How to Set Up a Slab-Based Pricing Rule

Switch to the Pricing Rules tab and click Add Config. Choose Quantity/Slab Pricing as the rule type, choose your product, and fill in the tier conditions (e.g., Min Qty: 10, Max Qty: 50, Value: 45000). Save the configuration.

⚙️ How Pricing Works at Quote Creation

Step System Action What the User Sees
1. Customer Selection System checks customer's state, group, and specific ID. Selected Price Book automatically applies in the background.
2. Product Selection System retrieves the price mapped inside the selected Price Book. The default unit price is pre-filled with the custom book rate.
3. Quantity Update System checks if the item quantity falls into any Slab Rules. If quantity goes from 5 to 15, unit price drops automatically to match the slab tier.
4. Applying Coupons Salesperson enters a promo code (e.g., OFFER10). Subtotal updates instantly with the percentage or flat discount.
